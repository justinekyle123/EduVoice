# TITLE PROPOSAL

## 1. Name of Proponent:

Necesario, Justine Kyle A.

## 2. Proposed Case Study Title

EduVoice: Voice Chat Learning Assistant with Quiz System

## 3.0 Area of Investigation

Polomolok, South Cotabato

## 4.0 Cited Problems

- **4.1** Students spend extra time chatting because the interface requires them to type every question manually instead of speaking it, and that typing time delays their answers and slows down their study sessions.
- **4.2** Learners struggle to get immediate answers while studying alone without an instructor because most learning platforms only offer static content and no real-time tutor to consult, leaving students stuck on confusing topics until their next class.
- **4.3** Students find it hard to interact with study materials hands-free while doing other tasks because most learning apps depend on typing and screen reading that occupy their hands and eyes, preventing them from reviewing lessons while commuting, exercising, or doing chores.
- **4.4** Learners spend excessive time converting uploaded documents into reviewable notes because current tools do not automatically summarize or restructure documents, forcing students to read entire files and write their own notes by hand and lose study time in the process.
- **4.5** Students lack a quick way to test their understanding right after reading a topic because existing platforms keep quiz creation separate from reading materials, so students have to draft their own practice questions and often skip self-testing altogether, leading to weaker retention.
- **4.6** Non-native English speakers, specifically Filipinos, struggle to use learning platforms that support only English because most AI-powered learning tools are built with English-only interfaces and language models, causing Filipino students who prefer Tagalog or Cebuano to misunderstand explanations or avoid the platform altogether.
- **4.7** Learners lose track of their study progress and remain unable to identify which topics need more review because learning platforms typically fail to record quiz results or study history across sessions, so students keep re-studying topics they already know while neglecting the ones where they are actually weak.
- **4.8** Students preparing for job interviews lack access to a low-cost, on-demand practice tool because professional interview coaching and mock-interview services charge fees and remain available only in fixed sessions, leaving students with limited finances to face real interviews without adequate practice.
- **4.9** Learners forget or lose track of study-related tasks and reminders because they rely on memory or scattered handwritten notes instead of a centralized tracking system, causing them to miss deadlines, review sessions, or exam preparation.
- **4.10** Students who receive direct answers from AI tools develop weaker critical-thinking skills because many AI chat tools give a complete answer right away without requiring the student to work through the problem, leaving them dependent on AI instead of practicing their own reasoning.
- **4.11** Learners with reading difficulties or visual strain remain underserved by text-only platforms because most learning platforms present content only as text with no audio or voice-based alternative, making it hard for students with dyslexia, visual impairment, or eye strain to access lessons and stay engaged in independent study.
- **4.12** Existing chat tools remain unable to detect a student's tone or emotional state during a conversation because they process only the literal content of a message without analyzing tone, wording, or vocal cues, so frustrated, confused, or stressed students keep receiving the same generic responses and may disengage from studying.
- **4.13** Students spend significant time manually creating quiz questions from their own notes because learning platforms do not automatically generate questions from uploaded notes or documents, taking study time away from actually practicing and reviewing the material.
- **4.14** Learners lack an accessible way to practice argumentation and debate skills outside the classroom because debate practice normally requires a live opponent, such as a classmate or teacher, who is not always available outside class hours, limiting students' opportunities to develop persuasive speaking skills before an actual classroom debate.
- **4.15** Students lack a single, centralized platform combining chat, voice, quizzes, and document-based learning support because current tools split these functions across separate apps, for example one app for chat, another for quizzes, and another for document readers, forcing students to waste time switching between apps and lose consistency in tracking their overall study progress.

## 5.0 Functions of the Proposed System

EduVoice will function as an AI-powered learning companion that will allow students to communicate with an intelligent tutor through text and voice. EduVoice will accept uploaded documents and will automatically generate summaries, question-and-answer sessions, and quizzes based on document content. EduVoice will detect the language and tone of student input, will respond in Filipino, Cebuano, or English as appropriate, and will adapt its guidance style through hints, direct answers, debate prompts, or simulated interviews based on individual learner needs. EduVoice will also track quiz results and interaction history to visualize each student's progress over time, and will capture voice-based to-do items, enabling learners to manage study tasks hands-free.

## 6.0 Feature of the Proposed System

### 6.1  Text-to-Speech AI Response

EduVoice will convert every AI-generated response into natural-sounding spoken audio, allowing students to listen to explanations instead of reading long blocks of text. The text-to-speech feature will help auditory learners retain information more effectively and will let students continue studying while performing other tasks, such as commuting or exercising.

### 6.2  Hint Mode

Once a student turns it on, Hint Mode will withhold the direct answer to a question and will instead provide progressive clues that guide the student toward solving the problem independently. Hint Mode will strengthen critical thinking and problem-solving skills rather than fostering dependency on the AI for answers.

### 6.3  Document-Based Question and Answer

Students will upload PDFs, Word documents, or lecture notes, and EduVoice will read and index document content, allowing learners to ask questions directly about the material. The system will generate answers strictly grounded in the uploaded document, helping students clarify difficult sections without needing to search the entire file manually.

### 6.4  Quiz Generation

EduVoice will automatically analyze an uploaded document and will generate a customized quiz including multiple-choice, true-or-false, and short-answer items based on the document's key concepts. Quiz Generation will save students the time otherwise spent manually writing review questions and will let students immediately test their comprehension of the material.

### 6.5  Filipino and Cebuano Language Detection

The system will automatically detect whether a student is typing or speaking in Filipino, Cebuano, or English, and will respond in the same language. Language Detection will make the assistant more accessible to students in Polomolok, South Cotabato, preferring their local language over English.

### 6.6  Debate Mode

In Debate Mode, EduVoice will take an opposing stance on a topic chosen by the student and will challenge the student's arguments with counterpoints and follow-up questions. Debate Mode will train learners to defend their positions with evidence and will improve critical thinking and public-speaking confidence.

### 6.7  Interview Mode

EduVoice will simulate a real job interview by asking role-specific questions through voice interaction and will evaluate the clarity, relevance, and confidence of the student's spoken answers. Interview Mode will give students a low-pressure environment to practice interview skills before facing an actual employer.

### 6.8  Voice-Based Question and Answer

Students will be able to ask questions and receive answers entirely through spoken voice input and output, without needing to type. Voice-Based Question and Answer will make the platform accessible to students preferring verbal communication or multitasking, and will support hands-free studying.

### 6.9  Hands-Free Task Capture

Students will dictate study-related to-do items, deadlines, or reminders by voice, and EduVoice will automatically log the items into a task list. Task Capture will let learners record tasks quickly while their hands remain occupied, such as while commuting, cooking, or reviewing physical notes.

### 6.10  Progress Visualization and Summary Trends

EduVoice will compile each student's quiz scores, study sessions, and topic performance into visual charts and trend summaries. Progress Visualization will help learners identify strengths and weaknesses over time and will let learners focus remaining study time on topics showing weaker performance.

### 6.11  Tone and Mood Detector

The system will analyze the tone of a student's text or voice input to detect signs of confusion, frustration, or stress during a study session. After detecting negative sentiment, EduVoice will adjust its response style, for example by simplifying explanations or offering encouragement, to keep the learner engaged rather than discouraged.

### 6.12  Smart Flashcard Generator with Spaced Repetition

EduVoice will automatically extract key terms and concepts from uploaded documents and quiz results to generate digital flashcards. The system will schedule flashcard reviews using a spaced-repetition algorithm that will show cards more often after a student answers incorrectly and less often once mastery is demonstrated, improving long-term retention.

### 6.13  Collaborative Study Rooms

Students will be able to create or join virtual study rooms where multiple students will be able to quiz each other, chat, and compete on shared leaderboards generated from the same uploaded material. Collaborative Study Rooms will encourage peer-to-peer learning and will make quiz review more engaging through friendly competition.

### 6.14  Accessibility Voice Navigation Mode

EduVoice will allow the entire application, including menus, quiz selection, and document uploads, to be navigated using voice commands alone. Voice Navigation Mode will make the platform usable for students with visual impairments or reading difficulties facing challenges with a text-heavy interface.

### 6.15  Adaptive Study Planner

Based on a student's quiz performance and progress trends, EduVoice will automatically generate a personalized weekly study plan that will prioritize weaker topics and will schedule review sessions and flashcard sets accordingly. Adaptive Study Planner will remove the guesswork from deciding what to study next.
